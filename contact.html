Contact PixelPlay Arcade

PixelPlay Arcade is a free collection of 35 original single-player browser games. We welcome bug reports, game ideas, and questions about the site.

What to tell us

When reporting a bug, please include: the game name, what you tapped or pressed, and what happened (for example, a win, a loss, or closing the game). That is exactly what we need to reproduce and fix it.

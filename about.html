About PixelPlay Arcade

PixelPlay Arcade is a free collection of 35 single-player browser games, made to be played instantly — no downloads, no sign-up, no account.

What you can play

  • Puzzle: 2048 Slide, Memory Match, Minesweeper Mini, Sliding Puzzle, Simon Pattern, Word Scramble Rush
  • Classic Arcade: Snake, Breakout, Pong vs AI, Asteroid Shooter, Flappy Flyer, Pac Maze, Block Drop, Bubble Pop, Neon Trails, Bomb Runner, Checkers vs AI
  • Reflex & Speed: Whack-a-Mole, Reaction Test, Aim Trainer, Stack Tower, Colour Rush, Fruit Slice Rush
  • Board & Cards: Tic-Tac-Toe vs AI, Connect Four vs AI, Blackjack 21, Dice Duel, RPS League, Battleship vs AI
  • Mini 3D & Racing: 3D Cube Runner, Car Dodge Racer, Ball Roll 3D, Space Blaster 3D, Endless Jump 3D, Drift Racer

How scores work

Every game has its own leaderboard. Your best scores are saved only in your own browser on your device. Rival names on the boards are pre-filled samples so the boards never look empty — they are not other real players yet. A future update may add a global leaderboard.

How the site earns

PixelPlay Arcade is free for players. In future, advertising may be shown on the site to support it; any ad network used will be named in the Privacy Policy when ads go live. No ad code is running on the site yet.

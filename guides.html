PixelPlay Game Guides

This guide explains every game on PixelPlay Arcade in plain language: what the goal is, how you control it, and one tip that actually helps. All 35 games are single-player, free, and playable in the browser with no download or account. Controls below are general — each game's start screen also shows its own short description.

How controls work across the site

On a computer, use arrow keys or WASD to move, the mouse to click and aim, and the spacebar where a game needs a single action such as jumping or shooting. On a phone or tablet, tap to select, drag where a game asks for it, and use the on-screen buttons that appear for movement games such as Snake, Breakout, Pong and Cube Runner. The fullscreen button in the game view fills the screen and is worth using on small phones. Your best score for each game is saved only on your own device, so it does not carry over to another device.

Puzzle guides

2048 Slide

Goal: slide numbered tiles on a grid so that two tiles with the same number join into one tile with double the value, and reach 2048. Control: arrow keys or swipes. Tip: keep your largest tile in one corner and build towards it, instead of chasing merges all over the board.

Memory Match

Goal: turn over cards two at a time and find every matching pair. Control: tap or click a card. Tip: remember where you saw a card even when you did not match it — that memory is the whole game.

Minesweeper Mini

Goal: clear the small grid without tapping a mine, using the numbers to work out where mines must be. Control: tap or click a square. Tip: start with squares that are clearly safe, and only guess when the numbers leave you no certain move.

Sliding Puzzle

Goal: slide numbered tiles into the empty space until they are back in order. Control: tap or click a tile next to the empty space, or use arrow keys. Tip: finish the top row first and do not break it while you work on the rows below.

Simon Pattern

Goal: watch a colour and sound pattern, then repeat it exactly, as it grows longer each round. Control: tap or click the colours in order. Tip: say the colours out loud or give them numbers in your head — a spoken pattern is easier to hold than a visual one.

Word Scramble Rush

Goal: unscramble the letters to make the hidden word before time runs out. Control: type or tap letters. Tip: look for common word endings first, such as -ing, -ed or -er, and the rest of the word often falls into place.

Classic Arcade guides

Snake

Goal: eat food to grow longer without hitting a wall or your own body. Control: arrow keys, WASD, or on-screen buttons. Tip: do not chase food across the whole board — plan a loop that keeps your tail out of the way.

Breakout

Goal: bounce the ball with your paddle to break every brick. Control: move the paddle with arrow keys or by dragging, or use touch. Tip: aim for the corners of the brick wall, because a ball that gets behind the bricks clears them much faster.

Pong vs AI

Goal: outscore the computer paddle by getting the ball past it. Control: move your paddle up and down with arrow keys or touch. Tip: hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to change its angle and make it harder for the computer to return.

Asteroid Shooter

Goal: shoot asteroids before they hit you, and survive as long as you can. Control: move and shoot with keys or touch. Tip: keep moving — a stationary ship is an easy target, and small asteroids are worth clearing early.

Flappy Flyer

Goal: fly through gaps in the obstacles without touching them. Control: tap, click or press space to flap. Tip: tap in a steady rhythm rather than in bursts — small, regular flaps keep you level.

Pac Maze

Goal: eat every dot in the maze while avoiding the ghosts. Control: arrow keys or swipes to steer. Tip: do not corner yourself — always leave an escape route, and use the maze edges to lose a ghost that is following you.

Block Drop

Goal: stack falling blocks and complete full horizontal lines to clear them. Control: move with arrow keys and rotate with the rotate key or button. Tip: leave a single-column gap for the long block, and clear lines regularly instead of stacking to the top.

Bubble Pop

Goal: shoot coloured bubbles from the bottom and match three or more of the same colour to pop them. Control: aim with the mouse or touch, then shoot — the preview shows the exact colour you will fire, and the aim line shows wall bounces. Tip: aim for bubbles that will drop a whole cluster, not just for the nearest match.

Neon Trails

Goal: leave a glowing trail and survive longer than your rival without crossing any trail, including your own. Control: steer with arrow keys or touch. Tip: do not box yourself in — wide turns keep more of the board open than sharp corners.

Bomb Runner

Goal: move through the maze, place bombs to clear obstacles and foes, and do not get caught in your own blast. Control: move with keys or touch, place a bomb with the action button. Tip: place a bomb, then move two squares away before it goes off — one square is not always enough.

Checkers vs AI

Goal: capture all of the computer's pieces, or block it so it cannot move. Control: tap or click a piece, then a destination square. Tip: do not trade pieces for free — a piece you give away for nothing is a piece you will miss in the endgame.

Reflex & Speed guides

Whack-a-Mole

Goal: tap moles as they pop up, before they disappear, and score as many as you can in the time limit. Tip: watch the whole board, not one hole — moles appear in more than one place at once.

Reaction Test

Goal: react the instant the signal changes, and get the lowest reaction time you can. Tip: keep your finger already on the button and do not tap early — an early tap counts against you.

Aim Trainer

Goal: hit targets as they appear, as fast and as accurately as you can. Tip: move your eyes to the next target before your hand finishes the current one.

Stack Tower

Goal: drop blocks one on top of another to build the tallest stable tower. Tip: drop each block when it is centred over the one below — a small overhang grows into a collapse a few blocks later.

Colour Rush

Goal: match the named colour under time pressure, ignoring colours that only look similar. Tip: read the colour name, not the colour of the text — that mismatch is the trap.

Fruit Slice Rush

Goal: slice falling fruit with quick swipes and avoid missing too many. Tip: slice through two fruits with one long swipe whenever they line up, instead of swiping at each one separately.

Board & Cards guides

Tic-Tac-Toe vs AI

Goal: get three of your marks in a row before the computer does. Tip: take the centre if it is free, and always block the computer's two-in-a-row before building your own.

Connect Four vs AI

Goal: connect four of your discs in a row, column or diagonal before the computer does. Tip: build two threats at once — the computer can only block one of them.

Blackjack 21

Goal: get a hand closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over. Tip: stand on a strong hand and do not chase 21 when the dealer is showing a weak card.

Dice Duel

Goal: roll higher than your opponent across the rounds. Tip: there is no skill in a single roll — the game is about playing the rounds, not outthinking the dice.

RPS League

Goal: win rounds of rock, paper, scissors against the computer and climb the league. Tip: change your choice often — repeating the same throw lets the computer's pattern beat you.

Battleship vs AI

Goal: find and sink the computer's fleet before it sinks yours. Tip: when you get a hit, search the squares around it before firing randomly again — ships are straight lines.

Mini 3D & Racing guides

3D Cube Runner

Goal: run as far as you can, dodging cubes and gaps. Control: move left, right and jump with keys or touch buttons. Tip: look two obstacles ahead, not just at the one in front of you.

Car Dodge Racer

Goal: steer through traffic without crashing, and survive as long as you can. Tip: stay in a lane with space ahead, and change lanes early rather than swerving at the last second.

Ball Roll 3D

Goal: roll the ball along the track without falling off. Tip: make small steering corrections — big turns are what push the ball off the edge.

Space Blaster 3D

Goal: shoot enemy ships in space while dodging their fire. Tip: keep moving sideways between shots, and prioritise the enemies closest to you.

Endless Jump 3D

Goal: jump from platform to platform for as long as you can without falling. Tip: jump a little early rather than late — a late jump from the edge is the most common fall.

Drift Racer

Goal: drift around corners and finish laps without spinning out. Tip: brake before the corner, not during it, and let the drift carry you through rather than fighting the steering.

Privacy Policy — PixelPlay Arcade

Last updated: 29 October 2026

PixelPlay Arcade is a free collection of 35 original single-player browser games. No account, sign-up or download is required to play.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location or any other personal information. The site itself has no login, no contact form and no server-side user database. We do not sell personal information, because we do not collect it.

Scores and leaderboards

Your best scores and play counts are saved only in your own browser (local storage on your device). Rival names shown on the leaderboards are pre-filled sample names so the boards never look empty — they are not other real players. A future update may add a global leaderboard; this policy will be updated before scores are ever sent to a server.

Cookies

PixelPlay Arcade itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage is used only to remember your own scores and play counts on your device, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising

No advertising code is running on the site yet. If advertising is added in future, this section will name the network before ads go live. When Google AdSense advertising is shown, Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and learn more at Google's advertising technology policy. We will never ask you to click on the site's own ads, and invalid clicks are against the advertising network's rules.

Third-party links

PixelPlay Arcade may link to external sites (for example, a share link). Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

The games are designed to be family-friendly and do not ask for any personal information from any player, including children. If you are a parent and believe a child has shared personal information through the contact page, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site changes (for example, when a global leaderboard or advertising is added). The updated version will be posted on this page, with the date above changed.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Contact page. The site owner's contact email will be listed there.

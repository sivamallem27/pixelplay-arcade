About PixelPlay — 35 free games, no download, no sign-up

PixelPlay Arcade is a free collection of 35 original single-player browser games, playable instantly on phone, tablet or computer. No download, no install, no account. The games are grouped into five categories: Puzzle games such as 2048 Slide, Memory Match and Word Scramble Rush for thinking and planning; Classic Arcade games such as Snake, Breakout, Pac Maze and Bubble Pop for fast, familiar play; Reflex & Speed games such as Whack-a-Mole, Reaction Test and Fruit Slice Rush for quick sessions; Board & Cards games such as Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four and Battleship against the computer; and Mini 3D & Racing games such as Cube Runner, Car Dodge Racer and Drift Racer for movement and dodging. Every game has its own leaderboard, and your best scores are saved only in your own browser on your own device — nothing is sent to a server, because there are no accounts. Rival names on the boards are pre-filled samples so the boards never look empty. The Game Guides page explains the goal, controls and one useful tip for every game, and the FAQ page answers the most common questions about playing, scores, safety and sharing.

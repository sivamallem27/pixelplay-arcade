PixelPlay Arcade — Frequently Asked Questions

Is PixelPlay Arcade free?

Yes. All 35 games are free to play. There is no payment, no subscription, and no purchase inside any game. If advertising is added in future to support the site, it will be shown on the page — it will never be charged to players.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. PixelPlay runs entirely in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Open the site, tap a game, and play. There is no app to install and no file to download.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no login, no sign-up form, and no password. You can play every game as a visitor, and the site does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information to play.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The site is made to work on phones as well as computers. Movement games show on-screen buttons on touch devices, and the fullscreen button in the game view makes a game fill a small phone screen. A steady internet connection is needed to load the site the first time; after that, each game runs in the browser.

How are my scores saved?

Your best score for each game, and how many times you have played it, are saved only in your own browser on your own device, using local storage. They are not sent to a server and they do not follow you to another device. If you clear your browser data, or switch to a different phone or computer, your scores start again from zero.

Are the leaderboard rivals real players?

No, not yet. The rival names on the leaderboards are pre-filled sample names so the boards never look empty. They are not other real players, and you are not playing against strangers. The Privacy Policy says this plainly. A future update may add a real global leaderboard, and the Privacy Policy will be updated first if that happens.

Are the games original, or copies of other games?

The games are original implementations made for PixelPlay. Some use classic ideas that have been around for decades — a growing snake, a paddle breaking bricks, a maze with dots — but the code and artwork here were not taken from other games, and no characters, music or artwork from other games are used. No game here is a copy of a commercial game.

Is PixelPlay safe for children?

The games contain no chat, no purchases, no gambling, no real-money prizes and no personal-data collection, which makes them suitable for children to play in a general sense. The site does not offer parental controls or age gates, so supervision remains up to the parent or device owner, as with any website.

Why does a game sometimes feel hard?

Each game has fixed rules and a fixed difficulty, and every player faces the same rules. There are no difficulty settings to change. Puzzle games reward planning, reflex games reward speed, and racing games reward timing — a game that feels hard at first usually gets easier once you learn its pattern, and the Game Guides page explains a useful tip for every game.

What do I do if a game has a bug?

Note the game name, what you tapped or pressed, and what happened — for example, whether it happened after a win, a loss, or closing the game — and send that information through the Contact page. That is exactly what is needed to reproduce the bug and fix it. Vague reports such as "the game is broken" are much harder to fix than a report with the game name and the exact action.

Can I suggest a new game?

Yes, suggestions are welcome through the Contact page. The best suggestions are original single-player browser games that can be played quickly, without an account, on both phone and computer. Suggestions that would need other players, real money, or copied characters cannot be added.

Will there be ads on PixelPlay?

Not yet. No ad code is running on the site today. Advertising may be added in future to support the free games, and any ad network used will be named in the Privacy Policy when ads go live. Players will never be asked to click ads, and clicking your own site's ads is never allowed — that rule is in the Terms of Service.

Who runs PixelPlay Arcade?

PixelPlay Arcade is an independent, single-developer site. The About page explains what the site is, the Terms of Service explain fair use, and the Contact page is the place for bug reports, game ideas and questions. A contact email will be added to the Contact page before any advertising application is submitted.

Can I share PixelPlay with friends?

Yes, please do. Share the main link — https://pixelplay-arcade-ten.vercel.app — and your friends can play immediately with no sign-up. Sharing the link, rather than copying the site's files to another website, is also what the Terms of Service ask for: please do not re-host PixelPlay's games as your own.
